How to order

Ordering

  1. Open a product and select your size.
  2. Tap Order on WhatsApp. WhatsApp opens with your order message ready. Just press send.
  3. We reply to confirm the product is available and tell you the total, including delivery.
  4. You pay, and we pack and ship your order.

Payment

We share the payment details on WhatsApp after we confirm your order.

Delivery

We tell you the expected delivery time on WhatsApp when you order.
