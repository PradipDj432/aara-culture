## Name and spelling
- Always **Aara Culture**: capital A, capital C, two words.
- In the logo the name is **AARA** (never "AC", never "Araa"). Some AI-made boards say "Araa" or "Cluture"; those are mistakes.
- Product codes start with **AC-** (`AC-001`); that's only a code, not a short name for the brand.

## How the brand feels
Graceful, calm and warm, with everyday clothes that look premium without shouting. Think a quiet boutique, not a busy market.
- **Website headline:** "Everyday *elegance*"
- **Brand quote:** "Simple, graceful clothing for every day, made to be worn, loved and worn again."

## Fonts (D-024)
Three free Google Fonts. Use **only these three**, everywhere.

| Font | Role | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Cormorant Garamond** | Titles | "Everyday elegance", page and product titles, "AARA" in the logo |
| **Montserrat** | Text | Paragraphs, prices, buttons, small uppercase labels, "CULTURE" in the logo |
| **Allura** (script) | Taglines | "Wear Your Story.", the brand quote, thank-you cards |

### Type styles
Every piece of text uses one of these styles. On the website they are CSS variables at the top of `css/style.css` (`--font-title`, `--font-text`, `--font-script`, `--size-*`, `--track-*`).

| Style | Font | Weight | Size on the website | Extras | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Cormorant Garamond | Medium 500 | 52–108px | Ivory on photos | Title on the photo slider |
| Title 1 | Cormorant Garamond | Medium 500 | 44–76px | — | Page titles (Shop, How to order) |
| Title 2 | Cormorant Garamond | Medium 500 | 32–52px | — | Section titles, product names on product pages, category names on photos |
| Tagline | Allura | Regular | 32–62px | Maroon | Taglines, the brand quote |
| Label | Montserrat | Medium 500 | 11px | UPPERCASE, letter spacing 0.22em | Menu, tabs, small headings, "WOMEN'S ETHNIC WEAR" |
| Button | Montserrat | SemiBold 600 | 12px | UPPERCASE, letter spacing 0.2em | Buttons |
| Text | Montserrat | Regular 400 | 15px (16px on computers) | Line height 1.7 | Paragraphs |
| Small | Montserrat | Regular 400 | 13px | — | Product names on cards, notes, codes |
| Price | Montserrat | SemiBold 600 | 13–18px | — | Prices |

Rules:
- Titles are maroon (ivory on photos), text is charcoal (or muted grey for less important text).
- Allura only for short lines, one sentence at most. Never for prices, buttons or long text.
- Italic Cormorant Garamond is allowed inside a title for one or two words ("Everyday *elegance*").
- For print (tags, packaging, cards), use the same fonts in the same roles.

## Photos
### Master image (D-025)
The **Olive Green Short Kurti** (AC-003) is the brand's main picture: the first photo of the home page slider (`images/hero.jpg`) and the link preview (`images/share.jpg`). Use it first for posts, ads and anything that needs one photo for the brand. To change it, replace `images/hero.jpg` and run `node brand/tools/export_png.js`.

### Photo style
- **Portrait (3:4)**, full length, the model in the centre of the frame.
- Warm daylight. Backgrounds: the palace courtyard (warm sandstone, arches, plants) or a plain light wall (cream, beige, light grey).
- The same framing for every product, so the shop looks tidy.
- First photo: the full outfit from the front. Second photo: a close-up or the back.
- On the website: JPEG, about 1000px wide, under about 250 KB, named `ac-XXX-N.jpg` (D-015).

## Website look (D-014, D-027)
- **Header:** logo on the left, menu on the right. On computers the links sit in a row (Shop · Tops · 2-piece sets · 3-piece sets · How to order) with the Instagram sign; on phones a ☰ button opens the menu from the right.
- **Home page:** a full-screen photo slider that starts with the master image. One photo fills a phone screen; computers show three side by side. It moves on by itself every 5 seconds and can be swiped. The header is see-through on top of the photos and turns ivory when the page scrolls. The photos are listed in `js/config.js` → `HERO_SLIDES`.
- Then: a short brand line under the Bloom, categories with their names on the photos, new arrivals, a photo with the brand quote, how to order, and an Instagram strip.
- **"Chat with us" button:** maroon, sharp corners, WhatsApp sign plus the words "Chat with us", bottom right. The product page uses its "Order on WhatsApp" button instead.
- **Shapes:** sharp corners (no rounded buttons or cards), thin 1px lines, lots of white space. Photos lead every page.
- **Icons:** thin line icons for menu, close and arrows; the official WhatsApp and Instagram signs (from Simple Icons, free to use), always in brand colours.

## Words
- Plain, short and warm. Talk to the customer as "you".
- Buttons say what happens: "Order on WhatsApp", "Shop the collection", "Chat with us".
- Prices: the ₹ sign with Indian commas, no decimals: ₹400, ₹1,000.
- Product names: colour + detail + type, in Title Case: "Olive Green Short Kurti", "Royal Blue Kurti Set with Dupatta".
- Sizes: S, M, L, XL, XXL.
- **Taglines from the owner's boards** (none confirmed yet): "Wear Your Story." · "Rooted in Grace." · "Tradition, Beautifully Worn." · "More than fashion. A culture you wear."

## Do and don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Use the logo files from `brand/logo/` | Retype the logo or redraw the Bloom |
| Spell it Aara Culture, and AARA in the logo | Write "AC", "Araa" or "Aara Cluture" |
| Use the five brand colours | Add new colours or bright WhatsApp green |
| Use Cormorant Garamond, Montserrat and Allura | Add other fonts |
| Keep sharp corners and thin lines | Use rounded buttons, heavy shadows or borders |
| Let the photos lead | Crowd a page with text |
| Keep Allura for one short line | Write paragraphs, prices or buttons in Allura |
